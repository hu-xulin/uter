# Pricing

Pricing is the live catalog of models and their prices. It is the authoritative source for what a request costs before you send it.

## Browse and filter

Each card shows the model name, provider and price tiers. Use **Filter** to narrow by provider or capability, and switch between **Card view** and **Table view**. The **Standard** badge indicates the pricing mode.

## Price basis

Prices are shown per million tokens (**/1M**) or per thousand (**/1K**) depending on the model, with separate input and output rates. Toggle the unit to compare models on the same basis.

<img className="block w-full rounded-lg border border-border dark:hidden" alt="Pricing filters" src="/user-guide/en/pricing/light/02-filter.webp" />
<img className="block w-full rounded-lg border border-border hidden dark:block" alt="Pricing filters" src="/user-guide/en/pricing/dark/02-filter.webp" />

## Model details

Open **Details** on a model to see its full rate card, supported capabilities and context limits. Use **Copy model name** to paste the exact id into your application.
