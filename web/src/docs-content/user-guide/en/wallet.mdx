## Top up

1. In "Add funds", choose a preset ($10 to $50,000) or enter a custom amount.
2. Check the payable amount (CNY) on the right.
3. Choose an available payment method (for example Alipay).
4. Complete payment; the balance arrives and can be checked at the top and in Order History.

The conversion shown on the page is authoritative.

## Order history

Order History lists top-up transactions and payment history, with search by order number and a page-size switch.

## Redeem code

Enter a code in the "Have a redeem code?" area and click "Redeem". The quota is added to your balance. Codes are usually issued by administrators or campaigns; verify the source before redeeming.

## Referral program

The bottom of the Wallet page shows the referral program: users who register through your link count toward your invites. The panel shows pending rewards, total earnings and invite count, and lets you copy the referral link.

<Callout type="info">
  Referral links and reward rules are configured by the platform; rewards stay pending until confirmed. Do not confuse a referral link with an API key — the former is only for invitations.
</Callout>
