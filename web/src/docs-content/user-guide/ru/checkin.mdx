# Daily Check-in

Daily Check-in grants a small random quota reward once per day. It is a simple way to earn a little extra credit for regular use.

## Check in

Press **Check in now** to receive today’s reward. Each check-in gives a random amount of quota; the page shows your total check-ins and cumulative reward.

## Calendar and history

The monthly calendar marks the days you have already checked in. Use **Previous month** and **Next month** to review earlier activity — a day can only be claimed once.
