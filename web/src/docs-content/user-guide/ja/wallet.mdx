# Wallet

The Wallet holds your credit balance and is the entry point for top-ups and subscriptions. Every request deducts from it; when it is empty, calls fail fast with an insufficient-balance error.

## Add funds

The **Add Funds** card offers preset amounts with their payment prices, plus a custom-amount field bounded by a minimum. Choose a **Payment Method** — the list is driven by server configuration and can include Alipay, WeChat, Stripe and others. The **Amount to pay:** line converts quota into the payment currency at the live exchange rate.

## Order history

**Order History** lists every top-up attempt, including expired ones, with its status (*Success / Pending / Expired*) and a searchable trade number. Quote the trade number in any support request.

## Subscription plans

The **Subscription Plans** card shows each plan’s quota, validity period, quota-reset schedule and purchase limit; a *Recommended* tag marks the admin’s pick. **Subscribe Now** starts the payment flow, and subscribed plans appear with an upgrade option where applicable.
